# Product Brief

## Working Name

Koma Planner

## Problem

People often know what they want to accomplish, but not the concrete sequence of small actions required to begin.

Traditional planning tools can be too heavy at the moment of ideation. A user may not want a full WBS, Gantt chart, or project-management workflow before they have even started.

## Product Idea

Koma Planner accepts a rough natural-language goal and turns it into a practical todo list.

The generated tasks are immediately usable: the user can check them off, edit them, add missing tasks, delete irrelevant tasks, and continue refining the list.

## Target Initial User

A solo builder, creator, developer, student, or small-team operator who wants to convert vague intentions into concrete next actions.

## Core Promise

Write what you want to do. Get a task list you can start using right away.

## Non-Goals For MVP

- Full Gantt chart support
- Enterprise project portfolio management
- Complex resource scheduling
- Team permissions
- Multi-tenant SaaS architecture
- Advanced reporting

## Differentiation

The product should optimize for the transition from vague thought to action, not for formal project management.
